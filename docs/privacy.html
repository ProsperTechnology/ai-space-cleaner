Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Applies to AI Space Cleaner, including its Microsoft Store edition.

Local scanning and cleanup

AI Space Cleaner reads local file and folder metadata, installed-app and startup entries, and selected registry entries to identify storage use and cleanup candidates. Depending on your selections, it can inspect caches, AI models, old projects, browser data and other supported files. Scanning does not upload your files, browser history, app inventory or cleanup results to Prosper Technologies.

Settings, selected folder paths, cleanup logs, registry backups and cached license information are stored on your computer. Logs and reports may contain local paths and app names. Review them before sharing them with support. Cleaning can remove data permanently or through the Recycle Bin depending on the type of item and your choices. Registry backups and the Recycle Bin do not provide a universal undo function.

Pro purchases and licensing

The Free plan does not require an account. Optional Pro subscriptions are sold by Gumroad. When you activate or revalidate Pro, the app sends the product identifier and your license key to Gumroad over HTTPS. Gumroad returns purchase and membership information, which can include your email address, plan, refund status and subscription status. The app stores the relevant license information locally. It also uses local Windows machine and account identifiers to bind cached license information to your PC; these identifiers are not included in the app's Gumroad verification request.

Gumroad handles checkout, payment details, receipts and subscription management under its privacy policy. Prosper Technologies does not receive your payment card number through the app. Network services receive connection information such as your IP address when contacted.

Updates, promotions and external links

Microsoft Store manages installation and updates for the Store edition under Microsoft's privacy statement. The direct-download edition can check the published Gumroad update feed. The Free edition includes built-in tips and Pro promotions. No third-party advertising network or behavioral analytics service is configured in this Store release. Links you choose to open are governed by the destination website's policies.

Your choices and retention

You choose when to scan and which cleanup operations to approve. Automatic cleaning is optional; in the Store edition it requires the app to remain open or running in the tray. You can disable it in Settings. You can deactivate a paid license locally, delete local logs and backups when no longer needed, and uninstall the app through Windows. Deactivation or uninstalling does not cancel a Gumroad subscription; manage billing in your Gumroad library. Purchase records held by Gumroad are managed separately under its policy.

Support and policy changes

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send payment card details or post license keys publicly. Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page.
